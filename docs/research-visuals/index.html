Research Phases

Phase 1: Obfuscation Testing — Can LLMs detect gamma patterns without temporal cues? (Validated: 71.5% detection)

Phase 2: Temporal Dynamics — Can LLMs perceive regime evolution over time? (In Progress)

Phase 3: Cross-Asset Generalization — Index vs. single-stock dynamics, intraday mechanics, "Scar Tissue" hypothesis (Planned)

Phase 4: Neuro-Symbolic Simulation — Agent-based modeling, GNN contagion networks, causal discovery (Planned)
